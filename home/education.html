AI in the
Classroom

By Christopher Guevara

AI, while not inherently bad for the education system, still has some major risks when being used by both educators and students alike. For students, the excessive use of AI could lead to a case of codependency, leaving the student reliant on whatever chatbot they are using. Students who use AI to get the answers of their assignments rather than go through the problem-solving process themselves usually have a harder time learning necessary skills than others due to not exercising their thinking and problem-solving ability. For a developing child or teenager, this is undoubtedly extremely harmful for their development, and in short,they will probably end up really stupid.
