AI and the
Economy

By Christopher Guevara

The effects of generative AI have already crept their way slowly but surely in the job market. Firstly, this can be seen most clearly with artists and web designers who are having their expertise taken by generative AI. Producers are losing their ground more and more, as the competition was already tight enough, but now many (especially small) producers are up against algorithms made to make more money. This makes a situation where the only competitors left are above-average, high-quality producers who may charge over the average person's budget, or AI-produced media at a fairer price with lesser quality without the human touch.
